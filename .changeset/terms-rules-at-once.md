A rollout may say a version binds at once and why (`atOnce`): `editorial`, a fix whose acceptance of the version before it carries over so nobody is asked again, or `no-accounts`, dated while nobody had accepted anything. Either is exempt from the six weeks and a day; every other rollout is held to them as before. A snapshot without the field reads as it did.
