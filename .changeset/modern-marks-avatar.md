The product marks and the avatar tints from the GPlatform Identity bundle, upstream. `@ghub/tokens` adds `--gm-mark-tide` to `--gm-mark-moss` with `--gm-mark-paper` and `--gm-mark-ink`, and the seven avatar tints `--gm-avatar-1` to `-7` with `--gm-avatar-ink`, theme-independent, as `m-mark-*` and `m-avatar-*` utilities. `@ghub/ui/modern` adds `Avatar` (the picture, or the initial on a tint picked from a stable id by `avatarTint`, the hash identity-chrome and Identity share; an optional badge such as a pencil), `ProductMark` (a product's mark or its initial) and `ProductChip` (the mark and the name in a pill). The tints are measured: the initial clears 9.5:1 on every one, and every mark hue clears 3:1 on white and on the dark ground.
