Console, a flat and dense theme for operator consoles modelled on UniFi Network: `console.css`, switched on by `data-console` on `<html>` as Aurora is by `data-aurora`. It restates modern's tokens (a near-black ground, panels a step lighter, one hairline, blue as the one accent, colour only for state, no shadows on surfaces), runs the type a step below modern's (13px body) with smaller radii, and adds the shell's sizes (`--gc-topbar`, `--gc-rail`, `--gc-row`) and two chart series colours. Inter and JetBrains Mono are used when the app loads them as `--font-inter` and `--font-jetbrains-mono`. Dark only for now.
