Privacy requests from GPlatform Terms: `handlePrivacyRuns` publishes the product's erasure catalogue, takes the runs staff queued for its surface, carries each out (or counts it, in a dry run) and reports back. `publishErasureCatalogue`, `takePrivacyRuns` and `reportPrivacyRun` are the same steps one at a time.
