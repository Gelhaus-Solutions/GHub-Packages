`PickerDialog` in `@ghub/ui/modern`: the popup behind a picker whose list piles up (D157, drawn for GPlatform Legal). The caller's `load` answers one page for the search, the facets and the page, so the server searches, filters and pages; the search applies on Enter and after 300 ms, and a load the query moved past is aborted. Choosing one, the primary names whom and Enter on the highlighted row confirms; choosing several, rows tick, the foot holds the chosen as chips from any page, and "Chosen only" lists them. Rows are columns the caller describes, scrolling sideways below `minWidth`; a row that cannot be chosen says why. The modal contract is Go to's: portalled, Tab trapped, Escape and the scrim close, focus returns.
